Before filling SLNs, the extension switches register.uw to this term (the selector at the top-left). Type it as the page shows it (e.g. Autumn 2026). Leave blank to register whatever quarter is currently displayed.

⚠️ Stored locally on this device, roughly in plain text. Your NetID unlocks email, grades, and finances — only use this on your personal machine and click Clear when you're done. The extension types these into the UW NetID sign-in page only; they're never sent anywhere else. UW's SSO hard-expires every 12h, so this is what lets an unattended run re-login. Duo still applies — choose "trust this browser" once so it's remembered ~30 days.

UW policy forbids automated submission. With auto-submit on, the extension fills SLNs, waits 1.5–3s, clicks Update Schedule once, and on failure tries the conflict-group backup exactly once. Max 2 attempts per alarm. Use a personal device that won't be banned in critical-path contexts.

"Test fire now" is a REAL registration attempt (same as the 6 AM alarm). If auto-submit is ON and your SLNs are real, it will actually register or drop. Turn auto-submit OFF (or use fake SLNs) for a dry run.
